# Commercial licensing

The VDWT beta binaries are free for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License](LICENSE-PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md) and for small companies,
including in closed-source products, under the
[PolyForm Small Business License](LICENSE-PolyForm-Small-Business-1.0.0.md). When the source code is released, it will also be available
under the AGPL-3.0. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Who needs a commercial license

You need one if your company (including its whole corporate group) has **100 or more people**,
or **1 million USD (2019, inflation-adjusted) or more** revenue in the prior tax year, and you
want to use VDWT beyond the free evaluation below.

Once the source code is released under the AGPL-3.0, a commercial license is what lets such a
company ship VDWT, or software, firmware or hardware built with it, **without releasing its own
source code**, or run it as a network service without doing so.

Typical cases: drone, camera, video-transmission and robotics manufacturers putting VDWT in
closed firmware or silicon; streaming and conferencing services; chip vendors building VDWT into
an IP block, FPGA bitstream or ASIC.

If your company grows past the small-business limits while using VDWT under the PolyForm Small
Business License, you need a commercial license from then on.

## Evaluation

Any company, of any size, may download and use VDWT **internally for evaluation** (testing,
benchmarking and prototyping, without shipping a product or offering a service to others) for
up to **90 days** at no cost. Continued use, or any use in a product or service, then needs one of
the licenses above.

## What a commercial license gives you

- Use and distribution of VDWT in closed-source products, with no obligation to publish your
  source code.
- A patent license covering the copyright holder's patent claims in VDWT, for the licensed use.
- Terms fitted to the product: per company, per product, or a per-unit royalty for hardware.
- On request: priority support, integration help, and early access to new versions.
- Contractual warranties and indemnification can be discussed case by case.
